# Building a voice guide

Collect a few genuine samples and specific corrections; **2 texts and 1 correction are enough for a clearly provisional first pass**. A stronger version benefits from 5–10 or more pieces, preferably including unchanged writing the author likes.

For each item, note its provenance:
- `AUTHOR_ORIGINAL` — author actually wrote it.
- `EXPLICIT_FEEDBACK` — verbatim preference or correction from author.
- `COEDITED_COMPARISON` — successive versions; approval not established.
- `PROPOSED_EDIT` — suggestion, not approved.

Do not claim that an agent suggestion represents author preference without confirmation.

1. Identify repeated decisions: introductions, specificity, structural rhythm, humor, closings, degree of technical detail, uncertainty.
2. Derive specific, falsifiable rules, each linked to evidence ID. Note exceptions.
3. Ask the author to correct the resulting rules (not merely endorse an abstract personality).
4. Keep one or more phrases that **must not be edited** to teach restraint.
5. Distinguish preferences that belong to a particular channel from general rules.
6. Create or revise `VOICE.md` using [the bundled template](../assets/VOICE.template.md).

Don't copy the typos or punchlines from evidence. Don't imply a model was trained or that the guide automatically persists across unrelated sessions.
