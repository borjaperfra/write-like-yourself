# Write a new piece using my voice guide

Paste this prompt along with your completed `VOICE.md` and your task brief.

```text
Write the requested piece using VOICE.md as editorial guidance, not as a list of expressions to copy.

Task: [what to write]
Audience and channel: [who / where]
Facts and constraints: [the actual facts, length, tone or desired action]

Start from a real observation, problem or argument grounded in the brief. Use specific details and appropriate technical language. Do not invent anecdotes, metrics, testimonials, events or results. Preserve uncertainty; if a key fact is missing, insert a clear [PLACEHOLDER] rather than bluffing.

Respect the guide's rhythm and dislikes, but don't mechanically insert jokes, catchphrases or dramatic endings. Keep it appropriate for this particular reader and medium. Deliver the draft, not a narration of your process.
```
