# Start here · Build your own voice guide

No coding, API key, or special software needed. You can use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or another assistant able to read your examples.

**You'll need:** ideally 3–5 things you wrote and a few edits, but you can begin with **2 texts and 1 correction**. That first guide will be provisional until you've provided more evidence. More examples can come later. Avoid private correspondence you don't have permission to share.

## Copy this into your assistant

```text
I want to create an evidence-based writing voice guide using my own texts and corrections.

First, ask me to paste at least 2 things I wrote (ideally 3–5). Then ask for any real editing examples I have (ideally 3–5):
- my original text (when available),
- my feedback or revised text,
- whether I approved the revision.
If I have fewer than 3 corrections or none at all, proceed but clearly label conclusions as provisional.

Analyze decisions rather than quirks. Separate direct writing, my feedback, AI suggestions, and coedited versions. Don't present a suggested edit as one I approved. Never invent examples, personal stories or preferences.

Produce a VOICE.md with: context and audiences; distinctive traits tied to evidence; rhythm; what I dislike; examples of things to preserve; guidance per format; editing rules; uncertainties. For every rule cite an example ID.

Show me the guide for review before treating it as final. Ask which rules feel wrong or overstated. Keep the result in the language I actually write in.
```

## Once you've got `VOICE.md`

Use [write](prompts/write.md), [edit](prompts/edit.md), or [review](prompts/review.md) with your voice guide and a task-specific brief.

## Before publishing your samples

Remove sensitive information, private messages, identifying details of third parties and confidential company material. This repository's [case study](examples/borja/README.md) shows how to label evidence rather than publishing entire conversation logs.
